# 🛡️ SOC Role in Blue Team

## 🎯 Objective
This room explains the defensive purpose of the Blue Team, where the SOC fits within an organization, and the career path of a Tier 1 Analyst.

---

## 📝 Summary
A Security Operations Center (SOC) is a core component of the Blue Team, acting as the central hub responsible for monitoring, detecting, investigating, and responding to an organization's cyber threats.

---

## ⭐ Key Takeaways

- Blue Team focuses on defending an organization.
- The SOC consists of multiple roles working together.
- A Tier 1 SOC Analyst performs alert triage and escalation.
- Career progression exists within defensive security.

---

## 👥 Security Hierarchy 

| Role | Responsibility |
| :--- | :--- |
| **CEO/CFO/Company Owner** | Focus on global business objectives |
| **CTO/CIO/CISO** | Lead company wide IT |
| **SOC Manager/Team Lead** | Manage a single team or department |
| **Analyst/Engineer/Red Teamer** | Perform technical tasks like log analysis |

---

## 🏢 Security Departments

* **Blue Team**: Defensive security experts
* **Red Team**: Offensive security experts (pentesters/ethical hackers)
* **GRC Team**: Governance, Risk, and Compliance specialists managing policies and regulations.

---

## 🤝 Blue Team Structure

### Security Operations Center (SOC)
* **L1 Analyst**: Junior members who triage alerts and pass complex cases to L2.
* **L2 Analyst**: Experienced members who investigate more advanced attacks.
* **Engineers**: Experts in configuring security tools like EDR or SIEM.
* **Manager**: Oversees daily SOC operations and coordinates the team.

### Cyber Incident Response Team (CIRT)
* **Forensics Experts**: Uncover hidden threats in disk and memory data.
* **Threat Intel Experts**: Gather data about emerging global threat groups.
* **Malware Analysts**: Reverse-engineer malicious code to understand behavior.

### Specialized Defensive Roles
* **DevSecOps**: Integrates automated security tools throughout the development cycle.
* **AppSec Engineer**: Maintains a secure software development lifecycle for products.
* **AI Researcher**: Studies AI threats and creates modern defenses against them.

---

## 📈 Advancing SOC Career

### Internal SOC vs MSSP

| Topic | Internal SOC | MSSP (Outsourced) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Scenario** | Protect one single company's infrastructure. | Protect dozens of different clients at once. |
| **Working Pace** | Generally calmer shifts with less time pressure. | Fast-paced with constant queues of urgent alerts. |
| **Security Tools** | Work with a few tools but master them deeply. | Work with dozens of diverse security platforms. |
| **Incident Practice** | Fewer major attacks encountered per year. | High volume of weekly attacks to learn from quickly. |

### 💡 Analyst Growth Tips
* **Learn from alerts**: Treat every single alert as a learning opportunity.
* **Think like an attacker**: Understand offensive tactics to defend better.
* **Verify everything**: Never assume; always check the digital evidence.
* **Get involved**: Voluntarily participate in active incident responses.
